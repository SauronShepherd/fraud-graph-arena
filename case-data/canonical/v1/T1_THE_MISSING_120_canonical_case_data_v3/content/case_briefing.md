# T1 — The Missing 120

## Purpose

The first redesigned Academy case introduces the Investigation Board, a guided Genie query, evidence pinning, protected actors, submission, score and the six endings. GraphFrames and Zingg are shown in their normal positions but remain disabled; their messages direct the player to T2 — Connections Lab.

## Case truth

Buster Bulldog was assigned to Register 2. The POS system records a manual no-sale opening under his active session at 21:14. The register closes 120 BSC short. The next morning Buster sends a message admitting that he took 120 BSC. Nell Newf is the protected reporter, Poppy Poodle is an irrelevant employee, and Biscuit Corner is the victim organization.

## Golden path

1. Identify the board areas and observe that GraphFrames and Zingg are locked until T2.
2. Select Buster and inspect provenance.
2. Read the directed ASSIGNED_TO relationship.
3. Quote and run: “Show the records connected to the 120 BSC shortage.”
4. Trace access, action, loss and admission.
5. Pin the required evidence.
6. Protect Nell.
7. Submit Buster as the only suspect.
8. Review the score and the six possible endings.

## Expected result

Primary culprit: Buster Bulldog. Protected reporter: Nell Newf. Ending: CLEAN_COLLAR. Score: 1000. Credits remaining: 4.
