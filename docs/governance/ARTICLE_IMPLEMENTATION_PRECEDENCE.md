# Article implementation precedence

The published body of an iteration is the implementation authority for that iteration. A teaser or preview in an earlier article is a historical plan and does not override the later published article.

This resolves two known previews:

- FGA 01's “Twenty Rooms and No Furniture” preview does not require twenty fake packages; FGA 01's published Academy walking skeleton is authoritative.
- FGA 02's “Evidence, Not Answers” preview does not require evidence; FGA 02's published empty-paper/empty-graph contract is authoritative.

FGA Iteration 03 means the published article **Screen Me Up Before You Go-Go** and its declarative frontend screen-system requirements. The legacy end-to-end build plan uses `I03` for persistence ownership and the M20 transaction/runtime kernel; those tasks are not part of article Iteration 03 unless separately scheduled.

FGA Iteration 04 means the Canonical Case Data Model v1.0.0 contract, its 32-table package boundary, typed registry, converters, and truth firewall. FGA Iteration 05 means the bounded canonical persistence and publication workflow over that contract. I04 and I05 may extend the infrastructure, but neither may change the public FGA 01/02 walking-skeleton promise or expose protected truth to WEB.

The FGA 00 constitutional list of twenty logical responsibilities remains registered in `config/governance/module-implementation-map.yaml`, with statuses describing only the current I00–I02 boundary or implemented subset. Existing backend authority, problem+json contracts, ACADEMY_001 identity, round lifecycle, empty board, accessibility, and no-closing-truth boundaries remain cumulative constraints.
